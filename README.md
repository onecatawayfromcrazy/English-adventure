English Adventure 📚⭐

An interactive Grade 5 CAPS English Home Language study app.

English Adventure makes studying more fun through interactive lessons, flashcards, vocabulary challenges, quizzes and unlockable practice papers.

Features

Interactive English lessons

Star rewards and progress tracking

Clickable word bank

Flashcards and topic challenges

Multiple practice quizzes

Three unlockable practice papers

Personalised learner names

iPad-friendly design

How to use

Open the app in your browser and enter your name to begin your English Adventure!

Progress is saved locally in your browser.

Made for Grade 5 learners following the South African CAPS curriculum.
